# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Startup founders, marketing directors, and product teams looking to hire or collaborate with an elite growth marketer who brings a mix of strategic vision and hands-on execution.

## Product Purpose
To showcase Alessandro Gentile's experience, skills, and case studies in growth marketing, driving high-quality inbound email contacts and establishing immediate professional credibility.

## Brand Personality
*   **Editorial Minimalism**: Generous whitespace, sharp typographic contrast, high-end editorial layouts.
*   **Expert Restraint**: Avoid loud animations and flashy generic AI aesthetics.
*   **Analytical Clarity**: Bold, clear statements backed by real data, case studies, and transparent workflow descriptions.

## Anti-references
*   **Generic AI SaaS Templates**: Pure white/neon blue pages with 3-column generic icon grids.
*   **Over-saturated animations**: Continuous bouncing, infinite fast marquees, or slide-ins that block content visibility.
*   **Marketing Slop**: Words like "supercharge", "empower", "seamless", or "next-generation".
*   **Scaffolded eyebrows**: Tracked uppercase labels (e.g., "SERVICES", "PROCESS") above every heading.

## Design Principles
1.  **Typographic Authority**: Make typography the main design element. Let large display headers dominate the canvas rather than decorative boxes or illustrations.
2.  **Harmonious Spacing**: Leverage the Fibonacci scale and Golden Ratio to define spaces, creating a natural sense of balance and breathing room.
3.  **Micro-Interaction Focus**: Limit motion to subtle, high-quality transitions (fade-ins, scroll reveals, and elegant hover adjustments) that guide the eye without causing visual fatigue.
4.  **No Placeholders**: Every visual asset, case study link, and logo must represent real, concrete details of Alessandro's work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
*   Strict WCAG contrast compliance (minimum 4.5:1 for body copy).
*   Keyboard focus states on all interactive elements (like the email copy button).
*   Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` settings.
